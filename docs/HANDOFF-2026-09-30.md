## 0. TL;DR of the current state

- The project had a **severe, previously unrecognized training-validity bug**:
  the mel resampler used the *batch-max* length, and the mel loss was *unmasked*.
  This corrupted every earlier run.
- After repairing it, a **conditioning-leverage control** proved the speaker
  branch can carry target identity (correct conditioning beats zero by
  +0.062, target-level 95% CI [+0.030, +0.099]).
- A mel-domain identity teacher **failed its gates** and was rejected.
- **Metric-aligned identity supervision** (frozen HiFi-GAN→ECAPA) works:
  V1−V0 = +0.017 [95% CI +0.010,+0.024], 14/16 targets.
- **Content suppression via K-means quantization of HuBERT L9** is the dominant
  lever: it collapses source similarity and flips the margin positive
  (attribution resolved in §7c; mean-norm contributes nothing to the margin).
- **Positive, significant zero-shot margin replicated across 3 training seeds:**
  VQ+id margins +0.0650/+0.0622/+0.0637, all target-level CIs exclude 0
  (16 held-out targets, n=240, fixed split).
- Cost: content fidelity (`content_L1`) degrades 0.85 → 1.02 with VQ;
  mel-correlation intelligibility proxy 0.83 → 0.72–0.74 (samples/).
- Buzz/twang is diagnosed (§7e) as decoder over-smoothing from the tiny local
  training budget — arm-independent; the scale-up is the primary fix.
- **Colab scale-up (train-clean-100, 247 spk, 40 held-out targets, n=624):
  margin +0.0941 for km+id, all gates pass (§7g).** km-only is now significant
  alone (+0.0643 CI excludes 0); the id effect doubled at scale (+0.0293).
- **Phase-2 id loss (full-length BOTH sides + anti-source hinge) added
  +0.0231 [+0.0161,+0.0299] paired → margin +0.1173 (§7h). Audition verdict:
  NOT audible — the id-loss shaping lever is audibly saturated; Phase-2 is
  canonical on numbers (no artifact cost), further loss-shape pushing is not
  expected to be heard.**
- **Two local conditioning/residual pilots (§7i) improved reconstruction and
  `sim_tgt`, but also increased `sim_src`; margin gains were only +0.0030 and
  +0.0075. Neither is ready for Colab scale-up.**
- **Raw-vs-vocoded ECAPA conditioning swap (§7j) is also negative:** the input
  embeddings differ measurably, but swapping in vocoded-reference embeddings at
  inference lowers margin and worsens `content_L1`; do not treat domain swap as
  the fix.
- **The current VQ+ID ECAPA branch is active (§7k):** correct conditioning beats
  zero by +0.078 margin and wrong-speaker conditioning by +0.135 on a 16-target
  screening set. Global token gain increases do not improve margin.
- **Block-specific token routing (§7l) was functionally initialized to the
  canonical mean and got gradients, but its matched fine-tune control showed no
  routing-specific gain. No Colab scale-up.**
- **The layerwise trace (§7m) shows ECAPA identity signal persists through
  speaker tokens, attention, decoder FiLM, and mel. The current trade-off is
  target identity vs content fidelity, not a disconnected speaker branch.**
- **Open:** next lever per §8 is a content-preserving cross-speaker objective;
  result JSONs should keep per-target rows going forward.

## 1. What the project is / hard constraints

Zero-shot voice conversion at a **hard <500k trainable-parameter budget**.
Frozen backbone: HuBERT-base layer 9 (content), ECAPA-TDNN (speaker),
SpeechBrain HiFi-GAN 16 kHz (vocoder). Trainable: content bottleneck, speaker
projection to 4 tokens, 4-head position-agnostic cross-attention, temporal
resampler, ConvNeXt-1D mel decoder.

Metric (on vocoded audio, SpeechBrain ECAPA):
`margin = cos(ECAPA(conv), ECAPA(tgt_ref)) − cos(ECAPA(conv), ECAPA(src))`
`sim_tgt`, `sim_src`, `content_L1` (mel L1 vs source) are reported alongside.
Vocoder ceiling ≈ 0.92 (cos between vocoded target and raw target).

## 3. The two training-validity bugs (root cause of years of false conclusions)

Both were in the training path; both are now fixed and the fixes are mandatory.

### Bug A — mel side: batch-max warp + unmasked loss
`HubertVCModel.forward` set `target_len = gt_mels.shape[-1]` (the padded batch
max), so every short utterance's features were stretched to the longest item,
and the mel L1 loss averaged over zero-padded frames. Quantified over 4,800
simulated batch-16 draws:
- resampler stretch (batch_max / true length): median **3.31×**, p90 **11.1×**,
  max 74×; **89.5%** of samples stretched >1.2×.
- zero-padding as a fraction of loss frames: mean **0.62**, median 0.70, p90 0.91.
- real log-mel mean is **−3.67** and padding is 0, so the model was partly
  rewarded for predicting zeros.

### Bug B — source side: content tail was warped in
`TemporalResampler` per-sample path did `xi = x[i:i+1]` (full batch-max content
`T`, including the zero-padded tail) then interpolated to the true mel length.
`content_lens` was collected but never passed to the model.

### Fixes (in place)
- `models/convnext_decoder.py` — `TemporalResampler.forward(..., target_lengths,
  source_lengths)` crops `x[i, :L_content_i]` **before** interpolating to
  `L_mel_i`.
- `models/hubertvc_model.py` — `forward(..., target_lengths, content_lengths)`
  threads per-sample lengths to the resampler.
- All callers pass both. `exp_content_factorial.py` is the canonical Colab path
  (`--device cuda`); the old `run_heavy_2hr_pipeline.py` is archived
  (`_archive/scripts/`).

## 5. Why the model was failing: objective identifiability + content leakage

Evidence chain (all on cached features / fixed eval):
1. **Conditioning was identity-neutral.** Training-free probe on `best_heavy`:
   cross margin +0.009 vs zero-speaker −0.003 vs random −0.006 (mel fingerprint);
   swapping the speaker changes the waveform (rel-change ≈1.5) but not whose
   voice it is.
2. **The content path leaks source timbre.** After the validity fix, the
   conditioning control on V0 shows the branch *can* work, but source
   similarity still exceeds target (`sim_src` 0.31 > `sim_tgt` 0.22).
3. **Content suppression is the lever.** VQ collapses `sim_src` 0.314 → 0.156
   and flips the margin positive.

### Conditioning control (the decisive causal test)
`diag_conditioning_leverage.py` — hold content fixed, condition on
correct/zero/random speaker, measure real ECAPA margin, bootstrap by target:

| model | Δmargin correct−zero | 95% CI | targets correct>zero |
|---|---|---|---|
| mel-fix only | −0.0107 | [−0.034, +0.013] | 6/16 |
| **V0 (mel+source fix)** | **+0.0624** | **[+0.030, +0.099]** | **13/16** |
| V0 correct−rand | +0.1046 | [+0.063, +0.149] | 15/16 |

**The source-length fix is what restored conditioning leverage.**

## 6. The identity signal: what failed, what works

### Rejected — mel→ECAPA teachers
A frozen regressor `mel → ECAPA` (trained on cached mels + ECAPA) was intended as
a differentiable target-identity loss. Two acceptance gates:
- **Gate 1:** held-out `cos(pred,true)` must beat the mean-embedding baseline.
- **Gate 2:** teacher-space margin must correlate with real ECAPA margin at r ≥ 0.6.

| teacher | Gate 1 (Δ vs mean) | Gate 2 (r, held-out) |
|---|---|---|
| raw ECAPA targets | −0.040 | 0.22 (0.36 on train-overlap) |
| vocoded-ECAPA targets (`cache_vocoded_ecapa.py`) | −0.063 | 0.29 (0.41 train-overlap) |

Both **fail**. A shared mel→ECAPA map cannot recover unseen-speaker ECAPA
*direction* (the mean embedding is a better predictor). **Do not scale this.**

### Works — true frozen HiFi-GAN→ECAPA supervision
`L_id = 1 − cos( ECAPA(HiFi-GAN(G(C_i, S_j))), ECAPA(HiFi-GAN(M_j)) )`
with target `ECAPA(vocoder(real mel))` precomputed in
`cache/vocoded_ecapa.pt` (1368 utts, 24 train speakers). Only the generator path
carries gradient; vocoder + ECAPA are frozen. Implemented in
`exp_v1_true_ecapa.py` and `exp_content_factorial.py --stage id`.

- V0 → V1: margin −0.0916 → −0.0741, `sim_tgt` 0.222 → 0.238, `sim_src` flat;
  paired **+0.0169 [95% CI +0.0100, +0.0242], 14/16 targets**.
- **Saturates:** V2 (w_id 20→50, +300 steps) = −0.0735 ≈ V1. More weight/steps
  do not help.
- **A content adversary (gradient reversal on the 64-d bottleneck) does NOT
  suppress source identity:** `sim_src` 0.3448 → 0.3444. Do not repeat.

## 7. The breakthrough: content factorial (seed 0)

`exp_content_factorial.py` — clean 2×2, all through the repaired path, same
split/eval pairs, n=240, 16 held-out targets:

| arm | margin | 95% CI | sim_src | sim_tgt | content_L1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V0 continuous | −0.0916 | [−0.149, −0.026] | 0.3140 | 0.2224 | 0.8454 |
| V1 continuous + id | −0.0741 | [−0.129, −0.013] | 0.3122 | 0.2381 | 0.8523 |
| VQ no-id | +0.0424 | [−0.008, +0.093] | **0.1562** | 0.1986 | 1.0210 |
| **VQ + id** | **+0.0595** | **[+0.0088, +0.1133]** | 0.1655 | **0.2250** | 1.0288 |

Paired, target-level bootstrap:
- **VQ − V0 = +0.1323 [+0.1081, +0.1608], 16/16** (content suppression).
- **VQ+id − VQ = +0.0176 [+0.0083, +0.0266], 13/16** (identity supervision on top).
- V1 − V0 = +0.0169 [+0.0100, +0.0242], 14/16.

`VQ` = per-utterance mean-norm of HuBERT L9 **+** K-means(256) quantization.
Effects are near-additive. **This is the first configuration with a positive
zero-shot margin whose target-level CI excludes 0.**

**Caveats (do not over-claim):** single seed; the VQ arm bundles mean-norm and
K-means (attribution unresolved); `content_L1` degrades; audio not yet auditioned.

### §7e — Buzz diagnosis (2026-09-25): NOT quantization; decoder over-smoothing

User report: buzz/twang **identical across all arms** (V0, km128/256/512+id,
smoothed variants); content intelligible; vocoded GT refs clean → vocoder OK
on real mels. `diag_buzz.py` on the 4 audition tuples:

| arm | L1 | L1low(0-27) | L1mid | L1hi | jitter(pred) | jitter(GT) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v0 | 0.798 | 0.966 | 0.678 | 0.750 | 0.280 | 0.543 |
| km256_id | 0.912 | 1.038 | 0.832 | 0.864 | 0.222 | 0.543 |
| km512_id | 0.913 | 1.023 | 0.832 | 0.883 | 0.218 | 0.543 |

- Predicted mel is **smoother than GT** (jitter ≈ half of GT) → the buzz is the
  classic HiFi-GAN reaction to an over-smoothed low band, not quantization
  artifacts. Codebook size / smoothing change nothing audibly.
- Root cause: decoder over-smoothing under the tiny local training budget
  (20 ep × 2880 utts). VQ arms add a uniform fidelity tax (L1 +0.11,
  worst in low band), but the artifact is arm-independent.
- **Implication:** the Colab scale-up (more data, longer training) is the
  primary fix. If buzz persists after scale-up: (a) band-weighted mel L1
  emphasizing low band, (b) fine-tune decoder only, (c) learned VQ w/
  commitment (§8.7). Do NOT chase content-representation fixes for buzz.
- Gotcha: `diag_buzz.py` operates on `[1, 80, T]` mels — `L = gt.shape[2]`.

### §7k — Current VQ+ID conditioning leverage and gain screen (2026-09-26)

`diag_vq_conditioning_leverage.py` used the local VQ+ID checkpoint and a

  [−0.0466,+0.0119].

### §7l — Block-specific speaker-token routing (2026-09-27): no routing gain

The decoder normally averages the four ECAPA tokens and sends the same 64-D
vector to all four FiLM blocks. The ablation added a learned `4×4` softmax
routing table—**16 parameters**, zero-initialized so its starting behavior is
exactly the existing uniform mean. Local verification found **max output diff
0.0** at initialization and nonzero finite router gradients. Total trainable
parameters are 494,528.

The routed and no-router arms were both fine-tuned for 400 steps from the same
local VQ+ID checkpoint, using the same seed, data sampling, and objective; the
no-router arm is the matched control:

| comparison | margin Δ [95% paired CI] | sim_tgt Δ | sim_src Δ | content_L1 Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| no-router fine-tune − initial | +0.0126 [+0.0058,+0.0188] | +0.0169 | +0.0043 | +0.0064 |
| token-router − initial | +0.0116 [+0.0049,+0.0177] | +0.0190 | +0.0075 | +0.0062 |
| **token-router − no-router** | **−0.0011 [−0.0055,+0.0034]** | +0.0021 [−0.0013,+0.0056] | +0.0032 [+0.0008,+0.0057] | −0.0002 [−0.0024,+0.0016] |

The learned weights stayed effectively uniform: each remained within 0.0007 of
0.25. The small gain vs the initial checkpoint comes from the additional ID
fine-tune; routing itself does not improve the paired result and slightly raises
source similarity. **Do not scale this router.** A later decoder-capacity
experiment needs a different mechanism and a same-step no-router control.

### §7m — Layerwise identity-path trace (2026-09-27): signal persists to mel

`diag_identity_path.py` traces correct/zero/wrong ECAPA conditioning on one
fixed source-content example for each of 16 held-out targets (screening n=16,
not the benchmark n=240). It records speaker-token, attention, fused-feature,
per-block FiLM, decoder-block, mel, and vocoded-ECAPA changes.

Correct vs zero produced relative feature changes of 1.29× at speaker tokens,
0.58× at fused features, 0.68× at attention, about 0.95–1.01× in block FiLM,
0.45–0.49× in decoder blocks, and 0.18× in the mel. Target `sim_tgt` rose
+0.081 while `sim_src` was essentially flat (+0.003); `content_L1` cost was
+0.105. Correct vs wrong speaker similarly changed every decoder stage and
improved target-vs-source margin by +0.135 on this screening set.

The four normalized tokens have mean pairwise cosine 0.19; their mean-pooled
vector retains 0.63 of the average individual-token norm. Thus pooling reduces
the signal, but the control trace shows no stage where the target signal simply
disappears. Combined with §7l, the tested soft router is not the missing piece.
The local evidence points to a **content-vs-timbre trade-off in the mel
generator/objective**, not a disconnected ECAPA input. The next method should
preserve phonetic content while allowing the cross-speaker output to depart
from the source mel; it needs a local gate on `sim_tgt`, `sim_src`, and an actual
content/intelligibility measure before any scale-up.

Artifact: `diag_identity_path.py`, `identity_path_trace.json`.

## 12. Do-not-repeat list (with evidence)

- **Mel-domain identity teachers** (raw or vocoded targets): Gate 1 and Gate 2
  both fail (§6).
- **Scaling `w_id` / identity steps alone**: saturates (−0.0741 → −0.0735).
- **Content-bottleneck gradient-reversal adversary**: `sim_src` unchanged (0.3448 → 0.3444).
- **Trusting "L1 crushes speaker" / "Cross SPK_SIM ceiling ~0.20 is structural"
  from `docs/Journal.md`**: those conclusions came from bug-corrupted training.
- **4-speaker / 12-pair evaluation**: underpowered; baseline CI includes 0.
- **Any training with batch-max resampler length or unmasked mel loss.**
- **`exp_fromscratch_quant.py` as-is**: still has the length/masking bug and
  changes conditioning (`frames_only`) — confounded.
- **Auditioning metric deltas in the sim_tgt 0.22–0.25 range**: id-loss shaping
  (crop length, w_id/w_neg re-raises, more steps) produced three significant
  +0.02-margin steps that were all INAUDIBLE (§7h). Do not iterate this lever
  further; if identity quality must move audibly, the candidate lever class is
  conditioning capacity (§8 item 2) — and audition every step.
- **Scaling the current reference-frame or centered-residual conditioning
  pilots to Colab as identity fixes**: local paired margin gains were only
  +0.0030/CI crossing 0 and +0.0075, while `sim_src` rose in both (§7i).
  Preserve the fidelity findings, but a follow-up must show lower `sim_src`
  locally before any scale-up.
- **Replacing raw ECAPA conditioning with vocoded-ECAPA vectors at inference**:
  full-reference swap is negative/neutral on margin and costs content L1;
  crop160 is significantly negative on margin (§7j). A different train-time
  conditioning experiment needs a separate hypothesis and local gate.
- **Increasing the ECAPA token gain globally**: gain 1.5/2 reduces `sim_src`
  but reduces `sim_tgt` as much or more and worsens L1 (§7k). Do not repeat the
  global amplitude sweep.
- **The current zero-initialized per-block token router**: matched 400-step
  control showed no routing-specific margin or target-similarity gain; logits
  stayed near uniform (§7l). Do not scale this implementation.
- **Batched full-length L_id on free Colab**: OOMs 15 GB VRAM (8 full-length
  HiFi-GAN grad graphs held to one backward). `crop=0` uses per-sample
  backward by design; don't "optimize" it back to a batched backward.
- **`--mean_norm` with the scale-up km arms**: `cf_km100*` checkpoints carry a
  RAW-frame codebook (km-only arm ran without mean-norm); the codebook-space
  hard check will (correctly) reject a mismatched `--mean_norm` invocation —
  drop the flag rather than adding `--legacy_codebook`.

## 13. Gotchas for the next agent

- The shell can lose the venv after an interruption — always call
  `.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe`, never bare `python`.
- Long background runs launched via `Start-Process` sometimes die (machine
  sleep). Prefer foreground runs with tqdm, and `--save_every` for resumability.
- `evaluate_arm` selection is deterministic given `n_src/n_content/n_ref`, so
  arms are comparable only at identical settings.
- `diag_teacher_proxy.py --eval_speakers` must be **held-out** speakers for the
  checkpoint being analysed (the old default 2086,174,2277,7976 overlap V0's
  training set — three of the four are seen by the 24/16 split).
- Report `sim_src`/`sim_tgt`/`content_L1` with every margin; margin alone is
  misleading (see the valid→V0 comparison in §5).
- `evaluate_arm` result JSONs strip `rows` — per-target component means
  (sim_tgt/sim_src/L1) are lost post hoc. For future paired decomposition
  either keep rows in the JSON or dump per-target component means alongside
  `per_target` margins.
- Colab `/content` is ephemeral — every session needs re-upload of ckpts and
  re-cache of the vocoded targets. The upload bundle is `casivc_colab.zip`
  (code only, no dataset/checkpoints); remember `--cache_dir /content/tc100_cache`
  and `--target_file /content/cache/vocoded_ecapa.pt` on Colab invocations.
